SudokuBits privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

English ภาษาไทย 日本語

In short: your game stays on your phone. If you connect Google Play Games, your progress is also saved in your own Google account and your scores can show on public leaderboards. Ads are optional, come from Google AdMob and are not personalized. We have no server, no analytics, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

SudokuBits is a sudoku game for Android made by Jc Calayag, an individual developer ("we", "us"), who is the controller of the personal data described here. Contact for anything about your privacy: support.sudokubits@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

The app keeps these in its own private storage on your phone. We have no server, so we never receive them:

  • Your player name and the avatar you chose.
  • A photo avatar, only if you pick one. The app keeps only its own small copy (256 pixels), saved again without location or camera details. It uses Android's photo picker, so it never sees your other photos.
  • Your games, progress, stats, badges, EXP, points, coins, looks and settings.
  • The daily reminder, if you turn it on. It is a notification made by your phone; nothing is sent.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases all of this, unless it is also in your Play Games cloud save.

Google Play Games (optional)

Play Games is optional; every part of the game works without it. If you connect:

  • Your Play Games profile. Google gives the app your Play Games player ID, gamer name and picture. The app never receives your Google account's email or real name.
  • Cloud save. Your progress (the same items as above, including a photo avatar) is saved with Google Play Games Saved Games in your own Google account, so it can come back on a new phone. You can delete it in the app: Settings → Play Games → Delete cloud save.
  • Leaderboards. Your scores (points, times, counts, your best Master Trials run) are sent to Google Play Games leaderboards, which are public. Other players see your Play Games name and score. Each score carries a short tag with your country (from your SIM, or else your phone's region), the number of the avatar you chose (never your photo) and the date the score changed.

Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can change who sees your game activity in the Play Games app's settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The Shop offers optional rewarded ads: you watch one only when you tap "Watch an ad", and it gives you coins. There are no other ads. We ask Google for non-personalized ads only (with restricted data processing), so ads aren't based on your activity in other apps or websites.

When an ad loads or plays, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

DataWhy
IP address (Google may estimate a rough location from it)Showing ads, ad measurement, fraud prevention
Ad interactions (for example app launches, taps and video views)Showing ads, ad measurement, analytics, fraud prevention
Diagnostics (for example app launch time and performance)Analytics and fraud prevention
Device identifiers (the app set ID)Ad measurement, analytics and fraud prevention

This data is encrypted in transit. The app does not use the Android advertising ID. Google uses this data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. Ads appear only once you have seen a short notice in the Shop.

What we don't do

  • No analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no tracking of you across apps.
  • We never sell your data, and we don't share it for targeted (cross-context behavioral) advertising.
  • Nothing is sold for money in the app. Coins come only from playing and from optional ads.
  • We don't ask for your email, contacts, precise location or files.

Children

SudokuBits is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the leaderboards or cloud save, contact us and we will help remove the data we can reach.

How long data is kept

  • On your phone: until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Cloud save: until you delete it in the app or in your Google account.
  • Leaderboard scores and ad data: as long as Google keeps them under its own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • know what personal data is kept about you and get a copy of it;
  • have it corrected;
  • have it deleted or destroyed;
  • have it moved to another service;
  • object to its collection, use or disclosure, or ask us to restrict its use;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (for example by disconnecting Play Games or not watching ads);
  • complain to your data protection authority.

How to use them: most of your data is only on your phone, where you control it. For the cloud save, use Delete cloud save in the app. For your Play Games profile and ad data, use your Google account settings. For anything else, email support.sudokubits@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. Requests are free of charge.

Legal basis: providing the game you ask for (the data on your phone, and the cloud save once you connect) and your consent (connecting Play Games and choosing to watch an ad).

Google stores and processes data on servers in many countries, including outside your own.
