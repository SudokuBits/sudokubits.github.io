SudokuBits terms

Last updated: November 4, 2026

English ภาษาไทย 日本語

These short terms apply when you use SudokuBits, a sudoku game for Android by Jc Calayag.

Coins

  • You get coins by playing (for example a daily puzzle's first solve) and from optional ads in the Shop.
  • Coins are never sold for money, have no cash value, and can't be exchanged, transferred or refunded.
  • Coins are used only to get looks inside the app.

Looks

  • Looks (animated avatars, profile styles and the Supporter pack) are cosmetic: they change how the app looks, never how the game plays or scores.
  • A look you get with coins is yours to keep for good, and it comes back with your Play Games cloud save.
  • Without a cloud save, uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases your coins and looks; we can't restore them.

Fair play

Leaderboards are for scores earned by playing. Don't change the app or its data to get scores; Google Play Games may remove such scores.

The app

SudokuBits is offered as it is. We work to keep it running well, but we can't promise it is free of errors. Play Games and ads are provided by Google under Google's own terms.

Privacy and contact

How the app handles data is in the privacy policy. Questions: support.sudokubits@gmail.com.
