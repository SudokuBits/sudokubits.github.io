Playing

What happens when I lose all 3 hearts?

Each wrong number costs one heart, and undo never gives it back. When all 3 are gone the game ends: Out of hearts, with no EXP and no points. You can start the same puzzle again whenever you like.

Why can't I undo a hint?

A hint fills a cell with the right number, and it stays: undo skips it, and it still counts as used. That keeps the no-hint bonus fair. Most puzzles give you 3 hints; time challenges give none.

What does a saved game keep?

Everything. Your game saves after every move: the numbers, notes, undo history, time, hearts and hints. Pressing Back or closing the app only pauses it. A saved game opens paused, with the board hidden until you tap Resume.

EXP and levels

How is EXP worked out?

A first solve earns its difficulty's EXP: Easy 30, Medium 60, Hard 105, Very Hard 165, Expert 240, Professional 360. You get 20% more if you lost no heart and 10% more if you used no hint. The daily adds 50%. Killer gives 1.5 times as much and Samurai 3 times.

Why do replays give less EXP?

A puzzle you've already solved gives 10% of what the same game would earn as a first solve, so a perfect Easy replay gives 3 EXP. New puzzles stay the way to grow, and replays still count a little.

I reached level 100. Does my EXP still count?

Yes. Level 100 is the top for now, but every EXP you earn is kept and shown as "EXP past max". If an update adds more levels or ranks, you get them right away from the EXP you already have.

How do I unlock harder difficulties?

Earn EXP to reach new ranks. Medium opens at Novice (level 3), Hard at Solver (level 8), Very Hard at Strategist (level 17), Expert at Master (level 33) and Professional at Sage (level 60).

Points and leaderboards

What's the difference between EXP, points and coins?

EXP raises your level and opens harder difficulties. Points are your leaderboard score: every solve adds some, replays too. Coins buy looks in the Shop. They never mix: spending coins never takes away EXP or points.

How are points worked out?

Each difficulty has its points, from Easy 50 to Professional 1,200. A fast solve adds up to 50%, each lost heart takes away 20% and each hint 15%, and the daily adds 50%. A solve always keeps at least 10%. A replay scores half.

Why am I not on a leaderboard?

Leaderboards need Google Play Games and a connection. You show up on a board once you have a score there: Fastest, for example, needs all 10 time challenges of a difficulty cleared. Scores are sent after each game and when you sync.

Who's first when two scores are the same?

Whoever reached that score first.

Coins and the Shop

How do I get coins?

Solve the daily puzzle: 3 coins, once a day. You also got 30 welcome coins at the start. Once ads open in the Shop, each optional ad you choose to watch gives 2 coins, up to 10 a day. Coins are never sold for money.

Why didn't I get coins today?

Daily coins come once per date, for your first daily solve. If your phone's clock jumped forward by more than 6 hours, coins pause until the clock is right again, and turning the clock back never gives a date's coins twice.

Are my looks kept for good?

Yes. A look you buy is yours for good, even after updates. Connect Google Play Games so your coins and looks are saved online: then a new phone or a reinstall gets them back.

Daily and streak

Is the daily the same for everyone?

Yes. Each date has one daily puzzle, the same for every player, and it never changes. Its difficulty follows the day of the week, up to the hardest one you've unlocked.

How does the streak work?

Your streak counts the days in a row you solved that day's daily. Today doesn't break it until the day ends. Your fire grows with it: Spark from 1 day, Kindling from 3, Hearth from 7, Copper from 14, Lilac from 30, Blue from 60 and Star from 100.

What is wood?

If you missed yesterday's daily but solved the day before, you can add wood for 5 coins today to keep your fire lit. The missed day joins your streak but doesn't add a day to it. Wood can't cover two missed days in a row, and the offer ends at midnight.

Google Play Games and saving

What does Google Play Games save?

Your progress: your level and EXP, solved puzzles and stats, badges, coins and looks, games in progress, your name and your photo avatar. It is kept in your own Google Play Games account. Theme, sounds, music and their volumes, the reminder and the language stay on each phone.

How does saving online work?

Your Google Play Games account keeps one save for SudokuBits. Each time the app syncs (when it starts, after a game, or when you tap Sync now), it checks what changed since the last sync. If only this phone changed, it saves online. If only the online save changed, for example because you played on another phone, it loads that save. Two saves are never mixed: one save always holds all of your progress.

I play on two phones. What happens?

Connect both to the same Google Play Games account. They share one save: the phone you played on last saves it, and the other one loads it the next time it syncs. Let a phone sync before you play on the other one. If both changed, for example while one was offline, the app asks which save to keep.

Why am I asked which save to keep?

This phone and Google Play Games both changed since they last synced. Each card shows its level, coins, puzzles solved and when it was saved, and the newer one says Newer. The save you pick is kept and the other one is replaced, which can't be undone. Badges from both saves are always kept. Cancel changes nothing, and the app asks again next time.

I have a new phone or reinstalled the app. What happens?

Connect Google Play Games before you play, and your save comes back by itself. If you played first, the app asks which save to keep, with your Google Play Games save first.

How do I change accounts or delete my cloud save?

To change accounts, use the Google Play Games app. To delete your cloud save, open Settings, then Google Play Games, then Delete cloud save. Your progress on this phone stays.

Why are my old scores still on the leaderboards?

Each leaderboard keeps your best score. Deleting your cloud save doesn't touch your scores, so after a fresh start your old best stays until a new score beats it. To remove your scores, open the Google Play Games app, go to Settings, then the option to delete your account and data, and under Delete individual game data, tap Delete next to SudokuBits. This also deletes your cloud save for SudokuBits and can't be undone. Your progress on this phone stays.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Every puzzle, the daily, the challenges and the lessons work without a connection. Only the leaderboards, saving online and ads need one, and they catch up the next time you're online.

Modes, challenges and trials

How do Diagonal, Samurai and Killer work?

Diagonal: both long diagonals also hold 1 to 9. Samurai: five grids, and each corner grid shares a box with the center one. Killer: each dashed cage holds different numbers that add up to its sum. Each mode has a short tutorial with Jc on the Learn tab.

What are Time challenges and Master Trials?

A time challenge is a puzzle against the clock, with no hints and 3 hearts. Master Trials is a climb of 100 levels with 5 hearts and 10 hints for the whole run. Both are on the Challenge tab.

Can a badge be taken away?

Never. Once you earn a badge it's yours, through every update and sync.

Privacy and language

Does my photo leave my phone?

Only where you send it: in your own Google Play Games save once you connect, and in a share card you send or save. The app keeps one small copy without location data.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings, then Language, and choose your phone's language, English, Thai or Japanese. With your phone's language, the app reads Thai or Japanese on a Thai or Japanese phone and English everywhere else.
