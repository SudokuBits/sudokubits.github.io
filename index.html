Four ways to play

One grid, four kinds of puzzle

Every mode is open from the start, each with six difficulties from Easy to Professional.

Classic

The familiar rules: every row, column and 3×3 box holds the numbers 1 to 9.

Diagonal

Both long diagonals hold 1 to 9 as well, so every placement counts twice.

Killer

Follow the dashed cages and their sums. No number repeats inside a cage.

Samurai

Five overlapping grids: each corner grid shares one box with the center grid.

Each puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved with logic. No guessing needed.
